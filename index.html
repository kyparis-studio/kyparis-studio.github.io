Kyparis is a small studio that builds games, productivity apps, and business software, and ships them. It is Kevin Holloway's studio, from Gainesville, Florida.

The name is from the Greek kyparissos, the cypress: the bald cypress of the swamps around Gainesville, rooted in water and standing for centuries. A cypress does not choose the flood; it chooses to hold its ground, and it keeps growing.
