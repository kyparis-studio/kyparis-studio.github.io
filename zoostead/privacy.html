Zoostead — Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Zoostead is a game made by Kyparis. This policy covers the game on Mac, iPad, iPhone and Steam.

The short version: Zoostead collects nothing.

Zoostead was called Wildhold while in early testing. Only the name changed; this policy is otherwise the one in effect since November 1, 2026.

What the game collects

Nothing. Zoostead has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your current zoo and a few settings on your device. A zoo you save to a file goes where you choose. Both are included in your normal device backups, which are governed by your platform's privacy policy, not ours. Deleting the game deletes what it kept.

Purchases

If you buy the game or its unlock, the store you bought it from (the App Store or Steam) handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details or your identity.

TestFlight beta testing

If you play Zoostead through TestFlight, Apple may share crash logs and usage statistics with us, and anything you choose to send through TestFlight's feedback tools (comments, screenshots) comes to us as you submitted it. This collection is performed by Apple under the TestFlight terms; we see it only to fix bugs and improve the game, and we don't share it with anyone.

Children

Zoostead collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version ever needs to collect anything, this page will say what and why before that version ships, the effective date above will change, and the store's privacy label will change with it. No silent revisions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: zoostead@kyparis.dev
