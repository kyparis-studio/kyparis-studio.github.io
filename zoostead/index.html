Zoostead

Your zoo, your ledger, your legacy.

Zoostead is a zoo you run like a ledger and a studbook. Start with an empty plot and a bank loan, lay out exhibits block by block, and close every month with a report: what each exhibit earned and cost, who was born, who died, and why the guests came or didn't.

Every animal carries real genes. Keep the studbook, pair animals to keep inbreeding down, watch for the rare coats real populations throw, and breed at-risk species well enough to release them to the wild. Six biomes, forty species, one honest balance sheet. For Mac, iPad and iPhone.

Beta

Zoostead is in testing on TestFlight. It will be free to try, with one purchase that unlocks the whole zoo. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a zoo that went wrong: email zoostead@kyparis.dev, or use the feedback button inside TestFlight. Say which device you play on and, if you can, attach the saved zoo.

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from. To restore the unlock on a new device, choose Restore on the unlock page in the game.
